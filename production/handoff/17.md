# Level design handoff

The [Buried Forest plan](../../world/dungeons/abyss-buried-forest-plan.md) covers floors 11–20. Floor 11 introduces the Root Stalker at the arch. Floors 12 and 19 provide traversal breaks. Floor 20 reserves its capstone and the descent into the Lost Mines. The plan preserves the canonical biome boundary and treats the floor 20 encounter design as a proposal.

Authoring continues from the quiet clearing on floor 12. The floor 20 moves and its transition to the Lost Mines need design and implementation. Playtests in UE 5.6 remain pending.
