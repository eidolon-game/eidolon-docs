# Level design handoff

The [Buried Forest plan](../../world/dungeons/abyss-buried-forest-plan.md) covers floors 11–20. Floor 11 introduces the Root Stalker at the arch. Floors 12 and 19 provide traversal breaks. Floor 20 reserves its capstone and the descent into the Lost Mines. The plan preserves the canonical biome boundary and treats the floor 20 encounter design as a proposal.

Layouts through floor 18 passed offline validation. The [floor 17–18 brief](../../world/dungeons/abyss-suspended-trunks-double-clearing.md) specifies the rotating bridge and two-stage latch. The [floor 20 capstone brief](../../world/dungeons/abyss-floor20-capstone.md) proposes Riftroot Tangle and its transition to the [Lost Mines](../../world/dungeons/abyss-lost-mines-plan.md), floors 21–30. The [Sunken Kingdom plan](../../world/dungeons/abyss-sunken-kingdom-plan.md) continues through floor 40 with a walkable main route and optional dives. The [Crystal Lands](../../world/dungeons/abyss-crystal-lands-plan.md) propose tactile geometry and visual cues through floor 50. Playtests in UE 5.6 remain pending.
