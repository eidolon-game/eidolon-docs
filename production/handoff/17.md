# Level design handoff

The [Buried Forest plan](../../world/dungeons/abyss-buried-forest-plan.md) covers floors 11–20. Floor 11 introduces the Root Stalker at the arch. Floors 12 and 19 provide traversal breaks. Floor 20 reserves its capstone and the descent into the Lost Mines. The plan preserves the canonical biome boundary and treats the floor 20 encounter design as a proposal.

Layouts through floor 20 passed offline validation. The [floor 20 capstone](../../world/dungeons/abyss-floor20-capstone.md) uses Riftroot Tangle as the end of the Buried Forest. The next runtime link is the [Lost Mines](../../world/dungeons/abyss-lost-mines-plan.md), floors 21–30. The [Sunken Kingdom](../../world/dungeons/abyss-sunken-kingdom-plan.md) continues through floor 40, the [Crystal Lands](../../world/dungeons/abyss-crystal-lands-plan.md) through floor 50 and the [Necropolis](../../world/dungeons/abyss-necropolis-plan.md) through floor 60. Playtests in UE 5.6 remain pending.
