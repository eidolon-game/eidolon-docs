# Level design handoff

The [Buried Forest plan](../../world/dungeons/abyss-buried-forest-plan.md) covers floors 11–20. Floor 11 introduces the Root Stalker at the arch. Floors 12 and 19 provide traversal breaks. Floor 20 reserves its capstone and the descent into the Lost Mines. The plan preserves the canonical biome boundary and treats the floor 20 encounter design as a proposal.

The Radura obliqua and Terrazze dei rami layouts passed offline validation. The [Terrazze dei rami and Bacino delle felci brief](../../world/dungeons/abyss-branch-terraces-fern-basin.md) defines the optional Stalker encounters, ladder release and drain interactions. Floor 14 is the next layout to author. The [floor 20 capstone brief](../../world/dungeons/abyss-floor20-capstone.md) proposes Riftroot Tangle and its transition to the Lost Mines. Playtests in UE 5.6 remain pending.
