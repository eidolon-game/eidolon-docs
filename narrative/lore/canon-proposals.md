# Canon proposals — not approved
## P09-001: Human emergence
Prefer leaving the first human origin undated and unexplained: Origin establishes laws, not a named creator of humanity. Alternative: humans arose independently under those laws. Neither claim is player-facing until director approval; do not imply divine bloodline for Kael.
## P09-002: Five unnamed founders
Reserve five unnamed slots, not character IDs or names, until screenplay review. Proposed contrasting motivations: shelter, provisioning, medicine, knowledge preservation, freedom of movement. No assignment to gods or cities and no mandatory bosses.
## P09-003: Iris
Propose a civic healing tradition using the title priestess without naming gods publicly. Alternative: concealed old religion, requiring stronger disclosure controls. Do not place public pantheon worship in modern cities.
## P09-004: Regional cultural differentiation
Valdris harvest ledger; Ignareth apprentice ring; Thalassar rescue rope; Nordheim storm shelter; Solkar water allotment; Elyndor public readings; Aureth civic oath. Each is a proposed tradition, not a mechanic or approved historical fact.
## P09-005: Naming and localization
Use existing names unchanged; proposed naming conventions by region must not encode ethnicity or fixed alignment. Common speech translated into Italian/English; no invented complete language or fixed calendar until review.
