# Cosmology and historical continuity
## Canon
Origin precedes the seven gods and governs fundamental laws. Gods were generated as custodians, not creators of the universe (ch24). The actual mechanism of humanity's emergence is unspecified; ancient human worship and direct divine rule are established, not divine creation of humans.
The Heart of Eternity once balanced laws; Aurelion appropriated it to impose order. Seven adventurers stole it roughly 3,000 years before the campaign; the Aegis built fortified cities and protects humanity. The damaged Heart rewrites history when used for protection or restoration; ordinary memories and records adapt. Exposure to Heart energy can confer resistance (Eidolon). Kael's first descent awakens this resistance. The erased are excluded from current history, not necessarily annihilated: Oblivion preserves displaced lives and worlds.
Titans are divine energy containers targeting Heart-generated alterations, not biological parents. Death fragments them into Giants (5–40 m), then smaller monsters. The Guild captures monsters and recovers energy for defenses; the two sides perpetuate the cycle. Titans can reach hundreds of metres, ancient examples over a kilometre. No invented universal fragment count.

## Relative chronology
- Primordial era: Origin; birth of gods; Heart balances laws. Human emergence undated.
- Pre-wall era: humans worship seven gods; direct rule, periodic erasures.
- ~3,000 years before campaign: theft, Guild foundation, fortification; divine retaliation begins. Stairway sealed for three millennia.
- Day 0: Kael turns 19, registers, descends; in prior reality Valdris suffers Titan attack, Heart restoration creates history containing Noah.
- Day 1: Noah appears to Kael; family remembers three years with him.
- Ch4–10: containment truth, Giant cycle, wider world, Titan cycle, Vareth erased.
- Ch11–16: divine origin of Titans, pantheon, founders, theft, Heart damage, Noah's origin.
- Ch17–22: factions, Nhalor's explanation, displaced child, Oblivion, alternate Veyrs, Noah erased and recovered.
- Ch23–26: Stairway, Empyrea, Origin, divine war, final choice.
## End-state invariants
I: Kael controls reality, Noah restored, cycle repeats. II: Heart returned, Titans cease, alteration-born lives including Noah erased. III: Guild retains Heart and walls, Noah restored, truth public, discrepancies continue. IV: displaced lives made permanent, stable passages between worlds rather than indiscriminate fusion, unilateral rewriting ends, Kael mortal, Noah grows up.
## Proposed interpretation rules
(P) Treat records as viewpoint evidence rather than omniscient truth; preserve original evidence in the memory archive. Never infer that a lost person is dead from absence alone. No absolute calendar or cosmological creation myth established until director review.
