## Shared institutions and language (P)
Local councils may administer water, roads and markets under Guild security/resource authority; independent sovereignty is not established. Use common speech for gameplay localization, local vocabulary for trades rather than fully invented languages. No modern temples to the seven or priests naming them before ch12; Iris's priestess title needs a director-approved secular or concealed tradition. Ordinary species above are design proposals, not monster taxonomy. Avoid defining currency conversion or economic prices outside RPG ownership.
## Spatial contracts
Valdris interior → first wall → Giant belt → second wall → Titan territory and wider cities. Abyss: 100 floors, ten named bands from Catacombs to Heart of the Abyss per story. Stairway: 100 ascending floors, seven law themes; exact allocation unresolved. Do not assume one extra 100-floor playable dungeon per city.
