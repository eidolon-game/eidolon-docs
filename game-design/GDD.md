# Game Design Document — v0.1 draft

## Approved baseline
Single-player dark fantasy Action RPG, progressive open world, UE5, PC/PlayStation/Xbox. Kael Veyr, 19, explores Aetheryon; Noah and the Discrepancy form the emotional core.

## Proposed gameplay
Third-person camera; real-time melee and magic; dodge, block, lock-on; attributes and equipment; companion AI; quest and world state; memory archive. Exact values, classes and controls TBD.

## Content targets
10 acts, 26 chapters, 4 endings, 100 Abyss floors plus 100 Stairway floors. 80–100h campaign and 180–250h completion are aspirational, not validated.

## Vertical slice
Home without Noah → Guild registration → first Abyss floor → anomaly → dormitory → home with Noah. Success: playable with keyboard/mouse and controller, persistent quest state, stable frame time and narrative continuity.
