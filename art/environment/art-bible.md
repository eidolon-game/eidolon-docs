## Production contract (proposed)
Blender meters, UE centimeters; Z up; apply scale; 0.25 m snap, 1/2/4 m primary modules, pivot bottom center or documented edge for walls. Door clearance initial 1.2 x 2.2 m, subject to capsule and camera validation. PBR base color in sRGB, normal and packed ORM linear; opaque dielectric roughness varied by wear, metals metallic only where exposed; avoid baked lighting. Author UVs, texel density target 512 px/m general, 1024 hero, verify in engine. Nanite candidate for opaque static rock/masonry; separate simple collision, foliage and translucent assets require independent profiling. Lumen test indoor/outdoor transition, emissive stability and reflections; no performance claim. World Partition/HLOD ownership with World Building. Weather variation must not obstruct combat telegraphs. No third-party reference image redistribution.
## Narrative variants
Keep Veyr furniture anchors and doorway stable; Noah props appear after first descent and disappear in chapter 22, with room becoming storage. Vareth ruins replaced by vegetation, not simply faded geometry; no residual collision/navigation or map marker. Oblivion has no sun, and inhabitants are real, not spectral decoration. Final II empty chair, Final I restored family and recurring discrepancy. Save/Quest own exact conditions.

Original concept sheets, modular mesh UV/material review, measured scale and collision, import verification in UE5.6, Lumen/Nanite captures, traversal and streaming tests, revision regression, provenance register and director signoff. None of those runtime gates claimed passed.
